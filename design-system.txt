# DESIGN AND STYLE GUIDE SYSTEM: THE "ZERO-NOISE" UX PHILOSOPHY

## 1. CORE PHILOSOPHY
- **The Rule of One:** At any given moment, the screen asks the user to make one primary decision. The most dominant element is the primary input. Everything else is secondary and pushed back visually.
- **Progressive Disclosure:** A blank, pristine slate. The interface unfolds only as the user interacts with it. No dashboards with 15 features.
- **Anticipatory State:** The app remembers user rhythms (e.g., prepopulate regular choices before they even search).

## 2. THE "WHITE GLASS" UI EXECUTION
- **The Canvas:** Pure stark white (#FFFFFF). No off-whites, no greys in the background.
- **The "White Glass" Layers:** Deepness without shadows. We use transparency (e.g., rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85) over a blur effect). A floating menu is a white layer with a subtle blur effect layered over the underlying white content. A physical sense of depth without color.
- **Micro-Interactions over Visuals:** Magic comes from how it moves. Tapping a card flawlessly expands to fill the screen (hero transition) while the background fades, feeling like manipulating physical paper.

## 3. TYPOGRAPHY (Fonts)
Typographic Architecture dictates importance. The size of the text dictates importance, eliminating the need for colored buttons to draw attention.
Font Family (Inter or similar geometric variable sans-serif):
- Regular: Inter_400Regular
- Medium: Inter_500Medium
- SemiBold: Inter_600SemiBold
- Bold: Inter_700Bold

Headers:
- Massive, tight, and pitch black (#000000).
- h1 (Display): 64px | Bold | Tracking: Tight | Line Height: Tight (1.1)
- h2 (XXXL): 48px | Bold | Tracking: Tight | Line Height: Tight (1.1)
- h3 (XXL): 36px | Bold | Tracking: Tight | Line Height: Tight (1.1)

Data Points:
- Crisp, small, and medium grey (#737373).
- Data (SM): 12px | Medium | Line Height: Normal (1.4)
- Micro (XS): 10px | Medium | Line Height: Normal (1.4)

## 4. COLORS
**Strictly Black & White with ONE Accent**
- Background / Canvas: #FFFFFF (Pure stark white)
- Surface: #FFFFFF (Pure stark white)
- Surface Glass: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85) (Over blur)
- Text Headers: #000000 (Pitch Black)
- Text Data Points: #737373 (Medium Grey)
- Border / Lines: #E5E5E5 (Only when structurally needed)

**The Single Accent**
- Accent (Stark Electric Blue): #0055FF
- RULE: Used exactly once in the entire flow, only for the absolute final action (e.g., "Confirm Booking" or live "Queue Drop" notification). When that color appears, the user knows exactly what to do.

## 5. SHAPES, BORDERS & SHADOWS
- **Strictly No Shadows!** Remove all drop shadows. Use White Glass and Blur for floating elements.
- **Border Radius:**
  - SM: 8px (Small structural blocks)
  - MD: 12px (Standard containers)
  - LG: 16px (Buttons, main interactions)
  - XL: 24px (Cards, Bottom Sheets)
  - Full: 9999px (Pills, final action buttons)
