# DESIGN AND STYLE GUIDE SYSTEM

## 1. TYPOGRAPHY (Fonts)
Font Family (Inter):
- Regular: Inter_400Regular
- Medium: Inter_500Medium
- SemiBold: Inter_600SemiBold
- Bold: Inter_700Bold

Line Heights:
- Tight: 1.2
- Normal: 1.5
- Relaxed: 1.75

Headings (h1 to h8) & Body Sizes:
- h1 (Display): 48px | Bold | Line Height: Tight (1.2) - Used for major landing page headers
- h2 (XXXL): 32px | Bold | Line Height: Tight (1.2) - Used for section headers
- h3 (XXL): 24px | SemiBold/Bold | Line Height: Normal (1.5) - Used for subsection headers
- h4 (XL): 20px | SemiBold | Line Height: Normal (1.5) - Used for prominent card titles
- h5 (LG): 18px | Medium/SemiBold | Line Height: Normal (1.5) - Used for standard card titles
- h6 (MD): 16px | Medium | Line Height: Normal (1.5) - Used for primary body text, buttons
- h7 (Base): 14px | Regular/Medium | Line Height: Relaxed (1.75) - Used for secondary body text, inputs
- h8 (SM/XS): 12px / 10px | Regular | Line Height: Relaxed (1.75) - Used for captions, overlines, small labels

- Primary Bg: #E8EAF6 - Indigo tint for backgrounds

- Secondary Light: #5FE0D3 - Lighter teal/glow

Text Colors:

Border Colors:

Action & Emotional Trigger Colors:

Gradients:

## 3. SHAPES, BORDERS & SHADOWS
Spacing System (8pt grid basis):
- XS: 4px
- SM: 8px
- MD: 16px
- LG: 24px
- XL: 32px
- XXL: 48px
- XXXL: 64px
- Specific Usages: Screen Padding (20px), Gutter (16px), Card Padding (20px)

Border Radius (Shapes):
- SM: 8px (Small elements, subtle rounding)
- MD: 12px (Standard cards, inputs, moderate rounding)
- LG: 16px (Buttons, main modal contents, clear rounding)
- XL: 24px (Large cards, containers, soft organic shapes)
- XXL: 32px (Bottom sheets, large structural elements)
- Full: 9999px (Pills, badges, circular avatars, round buttons)

Shadows & Elevation (Depth):
- SM (Elevation 2): Soft subtle shadow (y: 2, radius: 3, opacity: 5%)
- MD (Elevation 4): Medium float shadow (y: 4, radius: 8, opacity: 10%)
- LG (Elevation 10): Large pop-out shadow (y: 10, radius: 20, opacity: 15%)
- Glow (Elevation 5): Teal glow effect for primary interactive/highlighted elements (radius: 10, opacity: 40%, #1FB6A6)

Component Specific Shapes & Styles:
- Cards: Radius XL (24px), White Surface (#FFFFFF), Medium Shadow (MD)
- Buttons (Primary): Radius LG (16px), Background Indigo (#1E2A5E), Centered Bold Text, Padding Vertical 16px
- Inputs: Radius LG (16px), Height 50px, Background Surface (#F8FAFC), 16px Horizontal Padding
